    As a general rule of thumb, if someone reads 200+ chapters of a story and doesn't have anything good or even neutral to say about it in a review, he's not really reviewing the story, he's just ranting. That is not to say the story is perfect, but it certainly has a lot of merit in my opinion, and as opposed to many other stories, this one starts a bit slow and gets better the longer it goes on. I'll just to address the case of the "Dragon girl" first

    Spoiler

    Shortly after the MC has a serious talk with his girlfriend about his dream of building a Harem (which she approved of with certain terms) he meets a 10, 000 year old Dragon goddess in a dungeon that has awakened from a 1000 year slumber or so, her status screen shows she's extremely promiscuous. Now she's literally hungry and also hungry for "Sausage", MC offers to sate her lust in return for safety and after the fact she wants to take the MC for herself, MC answers that he loves his girlfriend and won't abandon her, throughout the story she visits the MC occasionally for s*x, giving him some gifts, some advice and leaves, it is only implied she sleeps with other people (it's never seen on screen) but she always returns to the MC because he has s*xual magical powers and she's slightly intrigued by his powers, so they are kind of f**k buddies until she catches some feelings for the MC, and even though Gaia pinged a "two way poly relationship" at that point, she only sleeps with other girls that John has a relationship with. If you consider that "NTR" then so be it. 

    [collapse]

    Now for the actual review, now as the summery says the MC is a nerd who got video game powers one day, the basic premise The Gamer Manhwa but everything from the characters to the rest of the world setting, MC's build is very different and the MC is not as OP but still powerful, the caveat to those abilities that they actually take some time to develop, that also goes for the MC personality.

    MC probably starts off with one of the weakest personalities that I know of. He's timid, unwise and can barely talk without a stutter and is somewhat naïve, that alone will turn off a lot of people, but the thing is throughout the story his character actually develops, he becomes stronger, wiser, loses his naïve nature and becomes a bit more ruthless.
    He also develops a lot due to his relationships in the story, be it his girlfriend, Harem members, friends or enemies. But the thing is again, it takes him some time to develop, and he does make a lot of mistakes.
    That brings me to the characters in this story, which is one of it's strongest points, characters have actual personalities, motives and still continue to evolve as the story continues, the banter is also a very strong point of this author in general.
    Especially true when talking about Harem members, which interact a lot with each other and not only with the MC, which also brings me to another point, Although the MC is the only male member of the harem and is the "centerpiece" Harem, the members do interact with each other a lot, both s*xually and not.
    They are also not "discarded" as soon as the MC wins them over, which is fairly common in Harem stories.
    This also causes the story to have a lot of slice of life parts, they are very entertaining but some might find that off-putting.

    As for his powerset, for a large part of the story he's a fairly standard elemental summoner with some mage powers, that means he's somewhat squishy and weak but has a huge amount of mana and mana regeneration, his powers later on evolve beyond this basic build, that give him some control over his gamer mechanics, s*xual stuff and more.

    As for the journey itself, it begins small and grows with time, I won't spoil much of it here but the stakes do get bigger with each arc of the story, it's also a larger world and the MC sometimes stumbles on enemies that he can't really handle and he does suffer for it, but that also makes the story feel more realistic in my opinion, and as I said before he learns from those mistakes.

    Overall, if we go by the earlier arcs of the story I'd give it around 4.3 but as the story goes on the arcs really improve so I'd give it an almost perfect score if it wasn't for people giving it 1-2 stars. Give it a shot and you won't regret it if you have some patience.
