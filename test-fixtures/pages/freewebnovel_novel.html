    SUMMARY

    Used as a sacrifice by his own parents to save his twin brother, Ezra Ashenlocke awakens to a nightmare known as the Trial of Steel.

    A child-soldier operation that started with one command.

    “Assemble your guns. Kill or be killed.”

    He realizes this is no test. . .

    It’s a massacre.

    But unlike the others, Ezra gains the ability to see fragments of memories from a life lived in another world. The life of a weapons engineer, a hardened soldier who spent years designing tools of war in a different world from his.

    Those memories—the only path for his new body to turn from prey into predator.

    Now, Ezra must use everything he has learned, and everything he will learn to survive the trial and the dangers waiting far beyond it.

    In the land known as the Country of Alchemy, Britannia he begins his path to master the art of Gun Alchemy.
